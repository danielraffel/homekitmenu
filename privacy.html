Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Homebar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your privacy is fully protected.

Data We Collect

None. Homebar does not collect any data from you or your devices.

How the App Works

Homebar connects directly to your HomeKit devices through Apple's HomeKit framework. All communication happens locally between your Mac and your HomeKit home hub (Apple TV, HomePod, or iPad).

  • Your device data stays on your devices
  • No data is sent to Generous Corp or any third party
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No account required

Network Connections

Homebar only makes network connections for:

  • HomeKit communication: Local network connections to your HomeKit hub, handled entirely by Apple's frameworks

Data Storage

Your preferences (which devices to show, display settings, keyboard shortcuts) are stored locally on your Mac using standard macOS preferences. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Homebar uses:

  • Apple HomeKit: Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
